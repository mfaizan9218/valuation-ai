02 · Capabilities

A workbench, not a chat window.

01

Tier recommendations

Structured pricing tiers with target segments, feature lists, and rationale — generated from your project context.

02

Willingness-to-pay

Low / mid / high WTP bands in your project currency, calibrated against category and target market.

03

Competitor context

Named benchmarks with positioning notes and price points so recommendations sit inside a real market.

04

Scenario simulator

Drag price and elasticity sliders on top of any tier. Revenue, volume, and Δ% recompute in real time.

05

Multi-run history

Every analysis is versioned locally. Compare runs across providers without losing prior recommendations.

06

Risks & assumptions

Every recommendation ships with the assumptions it depends on, so you can pressure-test before shipping a number.

03 · Method

From blank canvas to a defensible number in three moves.

I

Describe the product

Name, category, currency, target market, positioning notes. Five fields, no wizard.

II

Run the analysis

Your key, direct to the provider. Structured JSON parsed into tiers, WTP, competitors, risks.

III

Simulate scenarios

Branch any tier into a what-if. Watch revenue respond to price and elasticity in real time.
